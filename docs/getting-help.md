# Getting help

Two channels, split by one question: **does answering it require looking at your account?**

## In public, on this repository

Things whose answer is the same for everybody, and is worth other people finding later.

- How the bot behaves, and how to make it do what you want
- What counts against a monthly allowance
- A bug: it responded, but the response was wrong
- Something you would like it to do

[Open an issue](https://github.com/williamsinitiativesltd/whatproblem/issues/new/choose). Please
keep account names, private repository names and anything else you would not put on a billboard
out of it.

## In private, by email

Anything we can only answer by looking at your installation. Email
[support@whatproblem.dev](mailto:support@whatproblem.dev).

- The bot did not respond to an issue on your repository
- Your allowance or usage count looks wrong
- A question about your own plan
- Anything you would rather not discuss where your competitors can read it

Tell us the account the app is installed on and, if it helps, a link to the issue. We can see our
own logs from there.

Charges, refunds and cancellations are handled by GitHub rather than by us: GitHub takes the
payment, so they are the only ones who can act on it. Manage a plan from the
[Marketplace listing](https://github.com/marketplace/whatproblem-ai-requirements-clarification-bot),
and take an unexpected charge to [GitHub billing support](https://support.github.com/contact).

Data protection requests go to
[dataprotection@whatproblem.dev](mailto:dataprotection@whatproblem.dev). See the
[privacy policy](https://whatproblem.dev/privacy).

## How quickly

Every account gets the private channel. Nobody has to discuss their installation in public to get
an answer, on any plan, including the free one.

What a paid plan buys is our attention sooner: paid plans are answered ahead of free ones, and the
top plans ahead of those. Free is answered when there is time.
