# Plans and limits

## What counts

**One issue costs one unit of your monthly allowance**, no matter how many rounds of conversation it takes.

Charged once, when the bot first responds to an issue. Not charged:

- Follow-up rounds. The bot asks you a question; being billed for your own answer would be a strange way to run this.
- The closing summary. That is the bot's decision to post, not your request.
- Issues opened by bots, or pull requests. Never looked at, never charged.
- The one-time introduction the bot posts when you first install.

So if an issue takes four rounds and a summary to resolve, it still costs you one.

## When the month resets

Allowances reset at the start of each calendar month, UTC.

## Running out

There is a 10% grace band above your limit, so you are not cut off at exactly the stated number. Past that, the bot posts a note on the issue and stops responding until the month rolls over or you move up a plan.

A conversation already in progress is never cut off partway through, even if you cross your limit mid-thread.

## Beta

WhatProblem is in beta. The first 100 installations receive **150 clarified issues a month, free**, for the duration of the beta period.

Paid plans are on the way. Beta testers will be given notice and offered a founder price before anything about their allowance changes.

## Changing or cancelling

Plans are managed through the [GitHub Marketplace listing](https://github.com/marketplace/whatproblem-ai-requirements-clarification-bot), not here. GitHub handles all billing; we never see your payment details. A charge you did not expect is theirs to explain and theirs to refund: [GitHub billing support](https://support.github.com/contact).

Questions about how the allowance works: [ask here](https://github.com/williamsinitiativesltd/whatproblem/issues/new?template=billing.yml).
